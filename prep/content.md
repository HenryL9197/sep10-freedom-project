# Content
Topic: **EMS**

## Overview/Introduction/Context
* What are current hardware and software the EMS industry use.
* What are problems that the EMS industry face, and what are possible future technology that can benefit EMS in response time and quality of care.

## Part A: existing technology

##### Software
* ImageTrend Elite
  * It is a software used by over 9,000+ agency that turns real time data into analytics that make critical decisions clear. It also aide in  EMS incident reporting and documentation that gives crews time back from typing up the paperwork whilst treating a patient, using voice-to-text technology, so they can focus on treating the patient more attentively.

##### Hardware
* Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD)
  * A Computer Aided Dispatch (CAD) is a laptop/tablet that EMTs/Paramedics use to manage 911 calls, log incident details, tracks real time GPS ambulance locations, and routes to the closet unit or hospital-type facilities.

## Part B: possible future technology
* Predictive Hospital Load Tracking
  * With the new influx of AI, we can use these tools track ER capacity, open beds, and staff availability across whole regions in real time, routing ambulances to hospitals that least likely to cause extended offload delays, as currently hospital emergency rooms across the country are heavily overcrowded, ambulances frequently face hours of "waiting in hallways" just for an ER bed to open up so they can transfer care of the patient, which means that the hours they spend waiting for a bed to open, can be used to help the community more efficiently.
