## Introduction and Context
I expanded my prior knowledge in HTML by incorporating FreeCodeCamp (FCC) lessons, with what I remembered during my intro to CS class during freshmen year. On top of the basic body, html, and head tags—I learned (a href=") which taught me how to add a link on to a phrase, without all the extra jumble of letters and numbers that comes with a link—essentially keeping everything short and organized. In addition to the (a href=") tag, FCC taught me how to add a link to an image which was pretty nice when you wanted to make an interactive website.

## Challenges I faced
Some challenges I faced during linking images were that I constantly forgot to include quotes to a link I wanted to add or forgetting to put a "<>" on the "img src". Whether it was an image or a phrase.
Here is an example of my mistake:

<a href="#">img src= https://cdn.freecodecamp.org/curriculum/cat-photo-app/relaxing-cat.jpg alt="A cute orange cat lying on its back."</a>

## Solution

Now an easy fix would be to add the "<>" but whenever I'm up at 11pm in the night doing my SEP assignments on FCC, I get frustrated and I honestly didn't know what was wrong. In the future I should either, do my assignments earlier in the day or copy and paste the first part (img src="") to remove the headache of constantly forgetting to add a "<>" or quotes.

## Takeaways

In the end, it isn't some complex problem I needed to fix within a code, it was more of a time management or external problem that I need to fix. I should be doing my assignments efficiently and in a timely manner—while also taking notes instead of flipping back through FCC lessons.
